A preview that becomes an annotation

Move the pointer over the boat, the tyre, the chair or the church: the dashed outline is a suggestion. Click to keep it.

While the tool is on the editor is disabled, so the page has the pointer: hover says where it is and setPreview() shows what would be added there. Zoom in (pinch, or Ctrl / Cmd and scroll) and the suggestion stays in place. Switch the tool off to edit what you kept.
